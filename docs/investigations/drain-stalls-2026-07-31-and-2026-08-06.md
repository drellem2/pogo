# The two drain stalls: Jul 31 was healthy work, Aug 6 was ONE unmerged branch — and mg-853a fixes only the first

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Work item:** mg-5472 (diagnosis only — no repair authorised, and none made here)
**Hypothesis under test:** mg-742a (shelved), *"a polecat with NO merge pending lingers
after `mg done` and silently extends every deploy drain"*
**Evidence window:** the 1800s of 2026-07-31 and the 7214s of 2026-08-06, read before
mg-853a's narrowing went live and made the old behaviour unreproducible.

All times are **UTC** unless a `+01:00` offset is shown. The box is on BST, so log lines
in `~/Library/Logs/pogo/` (deploy log) are UTC and git/reflog timestamps are `+01:00`.

## Answer, in four sentences

**Jul 31 stalled on three genuinely-working polecats.** None of them had pushed anything
when the 1800s budget expired; all three pushed 30–47 minutes later and merged in under
three seconds each. The reply recorded on the night — *"the drain did not hang, 3 healthy
polecats outran the budget"* (mail, 2026-07-31T02:32:20Z) — **is correct**, and is now
backed by measurement rather than by inference.

**Aug 6 stalled on exactly one polecat that DID owe the refinery a merge.** `cat-z37ad`
(mg-37ad) had `origin/polecat-z37ad` pushed and not contained in `main` continuously from
**2026-08-05T22:48:36Z until 2026-08-06T11:18:44Z** — through the whole 7200s window and
for 7h18m past its end. The other three blockers had pushed nothing at all when the budget
expired.

## Verdict on mg-742a

| Claim in mg-742a | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

| "the 2026-07-31 failure … may have been partly this defect" (stated in mg-742a as a hypothesis, not a finding) | **FALSE** | all three Jul 31 blockers pushed *after* the timeout and merged normally; none was finished-and-lingering |

mg-742a should be **unshelved as the record of a real defect** — it costs drain time and it
corrupts concurrency accounting, exactly as filed. It should **not** be unshelved as the
explanation of the deploy failures. Those are two different claims and only the first
survives the log.

## Verdict on mg-853a (live tonight)

mg-853a narrows the drain from *"all running polecats"* to *"polecats that OWE THE REFINERY
A MERGE"*. `merge_debt_of()` in `scripts/pogo-self-deploy` asks, per polecat worktree:
does `refs/remotes/origin/<branch>` exist, and is its tip **not** an ancestor of
`origin/main`? That ref is written by the polecat's **own push**, so its reflog is a
timestamped record of exactly the quantity the new predicate reads — which is what makes
this counterfactual measurable rather than argued.

**Replayed against the two stalls:**

| Night | wait-set under the OLD predicate | wait-set under mg-853a | outcome under mg-853a |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-31 | 3 at timeout | **0 at the very first poll** | drain clears in seconds; deploy proceeds |
| 2026-08-06 | 4 at timeout | **1, for the entire 7200s** | **still exit 7** |

So the answer the ticket asked for, in the form it asked for it:

> *"If some blocker DID owe a merge, mg-853a will not have fixed it and tonight will stall
> again. That is the outcome worth knowing before it happens."*

**A blocker did owe a merge.** mg-853a is a real and correct narrowing — it removes the
mg-742a population and it would have saved Jul 31 outright — but it would **not** have
saved Aug 6, and it does not close the failure mode. A polecat holding a pushed branch the
refinery cannot land still holds the deploy for as long as the refinery cannot land it,
which on Aug 6 was **12h30m**.

## Method

Four independent records, cross-checked against each other:

1. **`~/Library/Logs/pogo/pogo-deploy.log`** — the drain's own poll line (`draining: N
   polecat(s) still active`) every ~15 s, and the `pre-bounce mail-check schedules` line,
   which names one schedule per live polecat at drain start.
2. **`~/.pogo/events.log`** — `agent_spawned` / `agent_stopped` reconstruct the registry at
   any instant; `refinery_merge_attempted` / `_merged` / `_failed` give the refinery's side.
3. **git reflogs of `refs/remotes/origin/polecat-*`** — the push times. Each one checked to
   begin with an all-zeros creation entry, so a first-entry timestamp is genuinely the
   **first push** and not a truncated history.
4. **`mg show`** on each work item — repo, type, and whether the task was capable of
   producing a branch at all.

`pogod`'s own log is **not** usable for either night: `pogod.log.1` stops recording real
daemon output at 2026-07-31T21:17Z and the current `pogod.log` is 98% a second launchd job
flapping every 10 s against `Cannot acquire pogod lock … held by pid 4368`. Nothing in this
document depends on it.

### The one that owed: `cat-z37ad`, and why 7200s was never going to be enough

`mg-37ad` is the refinery per-repo-lane redesign. Its branch was pushed and unmerged from
22:48:36Z on 2026-08-05. What happened to it during and after the window:

| time (UTC) | event |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-08-05 22:48:36 | first push — `origin/polecat-z37ad` created |
| 2026-08-05 23:54:02 | push |
| 2026-08-06 02:26:27 | push (`0cec0e7`) |
| **02:27:46** | refinery attempt 1 → **failed 02:27:51**, rebase conflict on `ARCHITECTURE.md` |
| 03:53:51 | push, after the polecat resolved the conflict |
| **03:56:12** | refinery attempt → **in flight at the 04:00:23Z timeout**, fails 05:21:15Z (gate: `./build.sh`) |
| **07:03:30** | refinery attempt → fails 08:03:34 (gate) |
| 08:33:10 | push |
| **09:44:37** | refinery attempt → **merged 11:18:44Z** as `4f39ed7` |

`git merge-base --is-ancestor 0cec0e7 f36d293` → **no**: at the moment of the timeout the
pushed tip was demonstrably not contained in the `main` the deploy had just synced.

The debt was continuous for **12h30m**. A 7200s budget cannot span it, and neither can
14400s. The Aug 6 stall is therefore **not** a budget problem and **not** a lingering-polecat
problem — it is a single branch the refinery took four attempts and 12.5 hours to land,
while the drain waited on the polecat holding it.

Note the shape of that 12.5 hours: three of the four attempts ended in a **gate failure
after 60–94 minutes**, and the gaps between attempts are the single-lane refinery queue
serving other branches. mg-37ad's own subject line records the condition it was filed to
fix — *"12 MRs queued 70min behind ONE slot-holder"*. The branch that would have relieved
the queue was itself stuck behind the queue, and the deploy was stuck behind that.

## What this changes, and what it does not

**Settled.**

- The Jul 31 stall is explained: three healthy polecats, an 1800s budget, no defect. The
  night's own reading was right.
- The Aug 6 stall is explained: one polecat owing an unmergeable-for-12.5-hours branch. Two
  lingerers were present and cost roughly 50 minutes of the window, but removing them was
  tried live at 02:50 and the drain still timed out 69 minutes later.
- mg-742a describes a real defect and is not the cause of either failure.
- mg-853a would have fixed Jul 31 and would **not** have fixed Aug 6.

**Not settled, and deliberately not repaired here (mg-5472 is diagnosis-only).**

- ~~What a drain should do about a polecat whose branch the refinery genuinely cannot land.~~
  **RULED AND REPAIRED (mg-3a96, 2026-08-10).** pm-pogo ruled on 2026-08-07 that the question
  was malformed: `owes` is the correct reading of that state, but merge-debt is an *inverted*
  proxy for the question that matters — **would stopping this polecat LOSE WORK?** A branch
  pushed to origin is DURABLE and its polecat is the safest thing in the fleet to stop; the
  genuinely unsafe state is committed-but-unpushed, and that wait is bounded by the polecat's
  own next push rather than by a merge queue. `merge_debt_of` in `scripts/pogo-self-deploy`
  is now `durability_of`, and the Aug 6 body — one polecat, branch pushed, unlandable —
  clears the drain on the first poll instead of holding it for the full 7200s. That case is
  asserted verbatim in `scripts/pogo-self-deploy_test.sh` as THE AUG 6 REPLAY. The refinery
  no longer appears in the drain's predicate at all, which is the ruling's rule 3: a refinery
  outage can no longer become a deploy outage.
- mg-08e9 (claimed) keeps the `/agents/drain` response body for future drains. Everything
  above had to be reconstructed from four separate records because that body was discarded;
  with it, the whole table would be one file. This diagnosis was not blocked on it, but it
  cost the reconstruction in the Method section.

## Reproducing any line of this

```bash
# the drain's own poll counts and transitions
grep -c 'draining:' ~/Library/Logs/pogo/pogo-deploy.log

# registry membership at an instant
jq -r 'select(.event_type=="agent_spawned" or .event_type=="agent_stopped")
       | select(.timestamp <= "2026-08-06T04:00:23")
       | "\(.timestamp) \(.event_type) \(.agent)"' ~/.pogo/events.log \
  | awk '{ if($2=="agent_spawned") a[$3]=$1; else delete a[$3] } END { for(k in a) print a[k], k }'

# the exact quantity mg-853a's merge_debt_of() reads, historically
git reflog show --date=iso-strict refs/remotes/origin/polecat-z37ad
git -C /Users/daniel/research/onethird_program \
    reflog show --date=iso-strict refs/remotes/origin/polecat-5040

# proof a first-entry timestamp is a ref CREATION, not a pruned history
head -1 /Users/daniel/dev/pogo/.git/logs/refs/remotes/origin/polecat-y48d8   # 000…0 old value
```
