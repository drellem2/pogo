- **The nightly runner's "terminated (SIGTERM)" line now reaches the deploy log when the SIGTERM lands mid git step (mg-14fe1).** The TERM trap wrote to whatever stderr was at that moment, and inside a git step that is the step's stderr capture, which the exit trap deletes. A deadline-ended night therefore showed the watchdog's `deadline: ...` lines but never the runner's own record that it was terminated. The runner now saves its stderr on fd 8 when it arms its traps. The TERM trap writes to fd 8, and the exit trap restores stderr from it before it does any of its bookkeeping. Exit codes and the deadline behaviour are unchanged. As with any runner fix, it is not live until `pogo service install-deploy` refreshes `~/.pogo/bin/pogo-deploy.sh`.
