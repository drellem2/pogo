A mail-check fire whose terminal wake the wake-cycle policy declines is now dropped instead of mailed (`nudge_delivery = "suppressed"` on `scheduler_fire_delivered`), since a mailed "check your mail" is a turn with nothing to do; sweeps and one-shot reminders still fall back to mail. The prompts describe the `mg schedule` sweep as an hourly backstop, since `mg` opens elapsed snoozes on every call. docs/CONFIGURATION.md gains a "Quiet defaults" section naming each lever.
