- **gc judges the ignored build output in kept, dead-owner polecat trees and their scratchpads, report-first (drellem2/pogo#282, mg-1bfc3).** A dead polecat's tree is kept whenever it holds an uncommitted file, and until now that kept its whole cargo `target/` (~2.3G per enth tree) and its harness scratchpad with it. Every sweep now reports, per kept tree whose owner is not live, the paths git reports as ignored (git trees) or the `target/`, `.lake/build` and `node_modules` enth tracks nothing under (enth checkouts), plus the same names in the owner's scratchpads, with their size. It deletes them only with `[gitgc] prune_build_output = true` (pogod) or `pogo gc --apply --prune-build-output`. A non-ignored untracked file, pogod's `.claude/`, and anything holding a `.git` are never touched. The enth polecat templates' CONFLICT/RED re-make now checks out beside the polecat's own tree (`<worktree>-remakeN`) instead of `$(mktemp -d)/w`, so gc reaps it under the same gate as the tree; the old line left a full enth checkout plus its `target/` in `$TMPDIR` that nothing ever removed.
